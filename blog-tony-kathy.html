Starlight Traveler Feature: Tony and Kathy

April 11, 2025  ·  Starlight2Travel Team

← Back to Blog

Our Starlight Travelers of the month are Tony and Kathy! We love featuring our incredible clients and sharing their travel stories. Here's a bit about their family vacation aboard the Wonder of the Seas with Royal Caribbean.

The Trip at a Glance

  • Vacation Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
  • Ship: Wonder of the Seas
  • Travel Time: June 2024
  • Special Occasion: Family Together Time
  • Room Category: Balcony Cabin

In Their Own Words

Overall, how was your trip and the accommodations with Royal?

"The entire cruise was a real delight. It really could not have been better. The service at all levels was outstanding!"

Did you feel the upgrade to a balcony was worth it?

"Balcony room very comfortable and well appointed. The balcony was great to watch the sunsets and look at the ocean while at sea."

Would you cruise with Royal Caribbean again?

"Absolutely — without hesitation. We would recommend Royal Caribbean to anyone looking for an exceptional family vacation at sea."

Thank you so much, Tony and Kathy, for sharing your experience! Stories like yours remind us exactly why we love what we do. We can't wait to plan your next adventure! 🌟
