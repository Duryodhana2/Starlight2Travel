✦ Our Story

We'll Move the Stars to Give You a Magical Adventure

Starlight2Travel, Inc. was founded in 2019 with one simple goal: to give every traveler the vacation of their dreams. What started as a passion for helping friends and family plan unforgettable trips has grown into a full-service travel agency serving clients across the country.

With 25+ years of combined travel experience, our team has the knowledge, connections, and passion to turn your dream trip into reality — whether that's a romantic getaway on a Caribbean island, a family adventure at Disney, or a destination wedding on the beach.

We are proud members of KHM Travel Group and certified Sandals Specialists, giving our clients access to exclusive deals, insider knowledge, and the best resorts in the world.
